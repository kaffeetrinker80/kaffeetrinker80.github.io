Philosophy & Purpose

Lightweight, browser-based helpers designed for specific workflows. Data stays local, ensuring maximum privacy and performance.

JSON Portability Full control via local JSON import and export.
Offline Ready Single-file structure. Use tools even without a connection.
Serverless No backends or tracking. Maximum speed and reduced complexity.
🔒 Privacy Conform & Secure Your data is read strictly locally inside your browser. Nothing is sent to external servers or GitHub.
📱 Tablet & Mobile Experience On tablets, use the browser's zoom function (75-80%) to fit the interfaces perfectly.
